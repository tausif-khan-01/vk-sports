Size exchanges

Ready-stock items (without personalisation) can be exchanged for a different size within 7 days of delivery, as long as they are:

  • unused, unwashed and free of marks or odours,
  • with original tags attached and in the original packaging.

Exchanges depend on the new size being in stock. If it isn't, we'll offer another colour, a different product or a refund.

Personalised & custom items

Jerseys printed with names, numbers or logos, and fully custom team orders, are made specifically for you. They cannot be returned or exchanged unless they are damaged, defective, or different from the design you approved.

Please check spellings, numbers and sizes carefully on the mockup before approving — we print exactly what's approved.

Damaged, defective or wrong items

If your order arrives damaged, with a manufacturing defect, or isn't what you ordered, send us photos on WhatsApp within 48 hours of delivery. We'll arrange a free replacement or a full refund, including delivery charges.

How to request an exchange or return

  • Message us on WhatsApp with your name, order details and photos of the item.
  • We'll confirm whether the item is eligible and share the return address or pickup details.
  • Once we receive and check the item, we'll ship the replacement or process your refund.

For size exchanges, the customer pays the return shipping and we cover shipping of the replacement. For damaged or wrong items, we cover all shipping.

Refunds

Approved refunds are paid to your original payment method (UPI or bank account) within 5–7 working days of us receiving the returned item. Original delivery charges are refundable only when the item was damaged, defective or incorrect.

Cancellations

  • Ready-stock orders can be cancelled for a full refund any time before dispatch.
  • Custom orders can be cancelled with a full refund before you approve the mockup. After approval, production begins and the advance is non-refundable.
