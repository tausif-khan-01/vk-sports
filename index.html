Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?+

Pick a product, choose your colour and size, then tap Buy on WhatsApp. Your order details are pre-filled in the chat — just press send and we'll confirm availability, delivery and payment. Ordering several items? Add them to your bag and send the whole order in one message.

Is there a minimum order for custom team jerseys?+

Custom names and numbers are available on single jerseys. For fully custom team designs with logos, tell us your quantity using and we'll share pricing for your team size.

How long does delivery take?+

We ship across India. Ready-stock items are usually dispatched within 1–2 working days; custom teamwear time depends on quantity and design approval — we'll give you a date when you order.

How do I find my size?+

Measure around the fullest part of your chest and match it below. Between sizes? Go one up for a relaxed fit.

Can I return or exchange a product?+

Ready-stock items can be exchanged for a different size if unused and in original packaging — message us on WhatsApp within 7 days of delivery. Personalised items with names, numbers or logos can't be returned unless they arrive damaged or incorrect.

Which payment methods do you accept?+

UPI, bank transfer and other options are confirmed on WhatsApp when we finalise your order.
