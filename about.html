Our story

From Parbhani to every playing field

We started VK Sports in Parbhani, Maharashtra, with a simple idea: local teams deserve kit as good as the pros wear — without big-city prices or complicated ordering.

Today we design and supply jerseys, training wear and fully custom team kits for cricket, football, kabaddi, volleyball, basketball and more, delivered anywhere in India.

Ordering is as easy as sending a WhatsApp message. You talk to a real person who helps with sizes, designs and delivery — from a single jersey to a full squad.
